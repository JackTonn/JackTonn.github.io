## Summary

As team lead of the Avionics subteam for Cyclone Rocketry, I organized a group of interdisciplinary engineering students to create a flight computer. A flight computer, in this context, keeps track of the position, velocity, and acceleration of the rocket during flight with a combination of sensors. Specifically, we used GPS, barometers, and IMUs to keep track of the rocket. Our rockets also have airbrakes on them, which push out of the rocket to slow it down. So, I helped my controls team get the data that they needed to write a PID controller for those airbrakes to increase our chance of reaching a specific altitude.
